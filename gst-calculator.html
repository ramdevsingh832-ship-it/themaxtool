How to Calculate GST?

Enter your amount, select the applicable GST rate and choose whether the amount includes GST. The calculator displays the taxable value, tax amount and invoice total.

GST Calculation Formula

Amount Before GST

GST = Taxable Value × GST Rate ÷ 100.

Total Amount = Taxable Value + GST.

GST Inclusive Amount

Taxable Value = Inclusive Amount ÷ (1 + GST Rate ÷ 100).

GST = Inclusive Amount − Taxable Value.

Difference Between CGST, SGST and IGST

CGST and SGST generally apply together to intra-state supplies. IGST generally applies to inter-state supplies. The correct tax treatment depends on the transaction and applicable place-of-supply rules.
