How to compress an image online

Select a JPG, PNG or WebP image, choose a target size such as 20KB, 50KB or 100KB, then press Compress Image. Download the result when it is ready.

When should you use an image compressor?

Smaller images can help meet upload limits for online forms, reduce email attachment sizes and improve page loading. Always check the image dimensions and clarity required by the destination website before submitting it.

Frequently asked questions

Is this image compressor free?

Yes. The tool is free to use and does not require an account.

Can every image be compressed below 20KB?

Not always. The final size depends on the original image, dimensions, format and visual complexity. The tool makes a best effort and reports the actual output size.

Are my images uploaded?

This page processes the selected image in your browser and does not upload it to TheMaxTool's server.
